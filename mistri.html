How we know the fix is real

The proof is a number. The same SadakSense analyzer that found the damage scores the road again after the repair.

1

Before and after score

The road must improve by at least 2.5 points and reach 6.0 or higher on the RQI scale.

2

Same stretch, same place

The after photo must match the job’s location and time.planned

3

Citizens confirm

Complaints on that stretch are closed only when residents agree it’s fixed.planned

4

Ratings follow the work

Only verified repairs build a mistri’s rating and unlock bigger jobs.
