02Scoring Engine

The Analytical Process

  1. 01

    Predefined Categorization

    Classification into Smooth, Moderately Damaged, and Heavily Damaged states based on metadata labels.

  2. 02

    RQI Assignment

    Each category translates mathematically into a Road Quality Index, providing a quantifiable metric for analysis.

  3. 03

    Traffic Simulation

    Mapping road quality to estimated traffic speed and congestion multipliers using physics-based domain knowledge.
