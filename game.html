How to Play

A 90 second shift on one road with three lanes. Rough lanes pay more, the smooth lane keeps you safe. Inspect, dodge and keep your suspension up.

Controls

ADChange lane. Arrows, tapping a screen half, or swiping also work.
SpaceEScan. Tags potholes 40 m ahead in your lane. Five tags file a complaint.
HHorn and headlights. Slow vehicles in your way move aside if the next lane has room. If every lane is packed, nobody can move.
PMPause, and mute.

The three lanes

Left: Severe DamageRQI 2.1 · 14 km/h · hatched · pays x2
Middle: Moderate WearRQI 5.4 · 40 km/h · dotted · pays x1.5
Right: Optimal (Smooth)RQI 9.5 · 115 km/h · solid · safe but thin

Scoring

  • Combo. Tags, close calls and chai build a combo up to x5. Avoid vehicles and wrecks.
  • Speed combo. Stay in one lane and your speed climbs, up to x5 (about +85%). Any lane change drops it back. Potholes and crashes bleed it too.
  • Hazard pay. Rough lanes pay points every second, boost close calls and chai, and are the only place gold sanction tokens appear.
  • Shift orders. The three orders at top left pay bonuses. Inspect both rough lanes to finish them.
  • Damage. Low suspension means a smoking, scorched car. Chai repairs it.

Lane conditions are scenario values (RQI 2.1, 5.4 and 9.5), not live data.
